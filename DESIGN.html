DESIGN.md · v alpha · apps/mobile + apps/web

The rent figure, the verified badge, the Chat with Owner button.

GHOR sells trust, not delight. This page renders every token and component defined in DESIGN.md so a screen can be checked against the system at a glance, instead of re-reading the spec file. One accent color, native system type, four border radii, one shadow tier — nothing here should compete with those three moments listed above.

Source of truth: /DESIGN.md · applies to apps/mobile (Expo) and apps/web (Next.js)

14

Do & Don't

Do

  • ✓Reserve primary blue for one action per screen.
  • ✓Keep price-display alone and prominent.
  • ✓Pair the verified badge with the owner's name every time.
  • ✓Use the same status-pill colors on My Listings and the admin queue.
  • ✓Keep chat bubbles flat — no shadow, no tail.

Don't

  • ✕Add a fifth border-radius value.
  • ✕Add a card shadow beyond elevation level 1.
  • ✕Use success-green decoratively — verified/approved only.
  • ✕Design a hover-only interaction — there is no cursor on mobile.
  • ✕Invent a star rating before that decision is actually made.
